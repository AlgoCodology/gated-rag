# HR Staff Handbook (Staff Only)

Classification: INTERNAL. Owner: Human Resources, Director of HR Marloes Smit.

## 1. Working hours and leave

### 1.1 Working hours

A full-time working week is **38 hours**. Staff may work flexibly between 07:00 and 19:00. Core hours for meetings are 10:00-16:00.

### 1.2 Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **232 hours** of annual leave per calendar year (about 29 days), plus 8 university closure days: the days between Christmas and New Year, plus Ascension Friday. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata. Leave requests of more than 10 consecutive days must be approved by the department head at least 6 weeks in advance. Unused leave expires on 1 July of the following year.

### 1.3 Special leave

| Event | Paid leave |
|:---|:---|
| Own wedding | 2 days |
| Birth of a child (partner) | 5 days at 100%, followed by 5 weeks at 70% (via UWV) |
| Death of a spouse or child | 5 days |
| Death of a parent | 3 days |
| House move | 1 day per year |
| Short-term care leave | Up to 2 times the weekly working hours per 12 months, at 70% pay |

## 2. Remote work

Employees may work from home up to **3 days per week**, in agreement with their manager. A one-time home-office allowance of 450 euros and a monthly allowance of 2 euros per home-working day are provided. Working from abroad is allowed for a maximum of **20 days per year** inside the EU, with prior approval from HR for tax and social-security reasons. Working from outside the EU is not allowed without approval from the Executive Board.

Employees receive a **holiday allowance of 8%** paid in May and a **year-end bonus of 8.3%** paid in December. Salary increases follow the collective labour agreement; the next scheduled increase is 3.0% on 1 January 2026.

## 4. Performance and development

Every employee has an annual *R&O conversation* (Resultaat & Ontwikkeling) in the first quarter. Each employee has a personal development budget of **1,500 euros** per two years. Teaching staff must obtain a Basic Teaching Qualification (BKO) within 3 years of starting. Assistant professors are evaluated for the tenure track in year 4.

## 5. Absence and sickness

Sick employees must call their manager before 09:00 on the first day of sickness. After 6 weeks of absence, the occupational health physician writes a problem analysis. Employees receive 100% of their salary in the first year of illness and 70% in the second year.

## 6. Confidential: reorganisation and redundancy

The 2026 reorganisation of central services is expected to reduce the support staff positions by 38 FTE through natural attrition and a voluntary departure scheme with a payout of 1 month's salary per year of service (maximum 12 months). Compulsory redundancies are not planned. This information is not to be shared outside the Executive Board, HR and the Works Council until the plan is announced on 15 January 2026.
