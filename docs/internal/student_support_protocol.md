# Student Support Protocol for Staff

Classification: INTERNAL. For study advisers, student counsellors and teaching staff. Not for distribution to students.

## 1. Students in acute distress

If a student expresses suicidal thoughts or there is an immediate danger to their safety:

1. Stay with the student and stay calm. Do not leave them alone.
2. Call the **campus security emergency line, extension 3333** (or +31 33 555 0133 from a mobile phone). For life-threatening emergencies call **112**.
3. Inform the on-call student counsellor via the Service Desk. The on-call counsellor is available Monday to Friday 08:30-17:00.
4. Students can contact the national helpline *113 Zelfmoordpreventie* at 0800-0113, free and available 24/7.
5. Document the incident in the confidential support system within 24 hours. Do not store details in e-mail.

## 2. Signs of concern

Teaching staff should refer a student to the study adviser if they notice a sudden drop in attendance, repeated missed deadlines, visible distress or withdrawal. Referrals are made through the form "Zorgmelding" in the staff portal. The study adviser contacts the student within **3 working days**.

## 3. Handling fraud suspicions

An examiner who suspects fraud during an exam must:

- allow the student to finish the exam, and confiscate the suspected material;
- write a report on the exam-room form, signed by the invigilator;
- send the report to the Board of Examiners within **2 working days**.

Examiners may not impose sanctions themselves, and may not tell a student what sanction to expect.

## 4. Privacy

Information about a student's personal circumstances may only be shared with colleagues who need it. Student counsellors are bound by professional secrecy and may only share information with the student's written consent, unless there is an immediate danger. Requests from parents for information about an adult student must be refused.

## 5. Funding exceptions

The student counsellors can approve one-off hardship payments up to **1,500 euros** from the Rooted Fund. Payments between 1,500 and 3,000 euros need the approval of the Head of Student Affairs, Karin Dekker. Amounts above 3,000 euros are decided by the Executive Board.
