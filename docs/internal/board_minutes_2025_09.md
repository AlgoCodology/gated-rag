# Minutes of the Executive Board Meeting, 16 September 2025

Classification: STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL. Present: Prof. dr. Marieke van den Berg (chair), Prof. dr. Joost Hendriks, Ilse Bakker MSc. Secretary: Annelies Mulder.

## 1. Opening and approval of previous minutes

The chair opened the meeting at 10:05. The minutes of 19 August 2025 were approved without changes.

## 2. Budget 2026

Ilse Bakker presented the draft budget for 2026. Expected income is 412 million euros, expected expenditure is 421 million euros, which gives a deficit of **9 million euros**. The main causes are higher energy costs, the indexation of salaries and lower government funding per student after the cuts to the national higher-education budget.

**Decisions:**
- The Board will present the budget with a reduction of central service costs by 6%.
- Faculties must reduce their discretionary spending by 3%.
- The reserve for the new Eemland sports hall (14 million euros) is frozen until the Ministry confirms its subsidy.

## 3. Student numbers and enrolment caps

Joost Hendriks reported that the Bachelor's programme in Psychology received 1,420 applications for 450 places. The Board decided to introduce a *numerus fixus* of **450 places** for the 2026-2027 intake. The Board also decided to cap the Master's programme in Data Science at 180 places, as teaching capacity is exhausted.

## 4. Campus and sustainability

The Board approved the plan to remove gas heating from Building C and Building D by the end of 2027, at an estimated cost of 6.8 million euros. The rooftop solar installation on the Library building will be extended by 1,200 panels.

## 5. Personnel

The Board discussed the appointment of Prof. dr. Femke Brouwer as dean of FHLS for a second term (2026-2030). The decision was unanimous. The reorganisation of central services will be announced on **15 January 2026**.

## 6. Any other business

The Board noted a request from the student council to extend the library opening hours during the exam weeks. This is already scheduled for the next academic year. The next meeting takes place on 14 October 2025.
