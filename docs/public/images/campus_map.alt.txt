Schematic map of the Home University Eemland Campus. North of the main road Eemlandlaan: Building A (IT Service Desk, server room A0.01), Building C (Student Counsellors, room C0.12), Building D (Faculty of Science and Engineering) and Building L (University Library, 450 study places). South of the road: Eem Sports Centre (gym, pool, sports hall), student housing by Woonstad Eem (2,400 rooms), bicycle parking (6,000 spaces) and the bus stop for the shuttle to the Stadspoort Site. Main entrance at Eemlandlaan 1.
