# Student Handbook 2025-2026

Welcome to Home University! This handbook explains the most important services and rules for students.

## Student services

### Student counsellors and study advisers

Every student has a **study adviser** at their faculty for questions about the programme, planning and BSA. For personal problems such as stress, financial difficulties or functional impairments, students can contact the **student counsellors** (Eemland Campus, Building C, room C0.12). Appointments are free and confidential. Walk-in hours are Monday to Thursday, 10:00-12:00.

### Library

The University Library (Eemland Campus, Building L) holds 780,000 printed volumes and offers access to more than 120,000 e-journals. Opening hours during teaching periods are 08:00-23:00 on weekdays and 10:00-18:00 on weekends. During exam weeks the library is open 24 hours a day. Students can reserve one of 450 study places via the library app, up to 7 days in advance, for a maximum of 4 hours per day. Books can be borrowed for 28 days and renewed twice.

### Sports centre

The *Eem Sports Centre* offers over 60 activities. The annual sports card costs 95 euros for students and gives access to the gym, the pool and all group classes.

### Housing

Home University works with the housing corporation *Woonstad Eem* to provide around 2,400 rooms. International students who apply before 1 June are guaranteed a room in their first year. Rents range from 520 to 780 euros per month.

## IT services for students

### Accounts and Wi-Fi

Every student receives a **HomeID** account at enrolment. Use it for the learning platform, e-mail and the *eduroam* Wi-Fi network. Passwords expire every 12 months. Students can reset the password themselves at the self-service portal; the IT Service Desk (Building A, ground floor) can help in person.

### Printing

Printing costs 5 cents per black-and-white page and 25 cents per colour page. Every student receives 100 free pages per year.

## Practical information

### Bicycles

The campus has over 6,000 bicycle parking spaces. Bicycles left in the wrong place or without a valid sticker may be removed after two weeks. Removed bikes can be collected at the Facilities desk for a fee of 10 euros.

### Student card

The student card is also the key to buildings and the library. If you lose it, report this immediately at the Service Desk. A replacement costs 15 euros.

### Complaints

Students who want to file a complaint, for example about undesirable behaviour, can contact the **Confidential Adviser** or the Complaints Committee. Complaints about grades and exams are handled through the appeal procedure in the Education and Examination Regulations.
