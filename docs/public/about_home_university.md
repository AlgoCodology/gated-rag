# About Home University

Home University (Dutch: *Thuis Universiteit*) is a public research university in Amersfoort, in the province of Utrecht, the Netherlands. It was founded in 1961 as the "Eemland Institute for Technology and Teaching" and received full university status in 1986. In the academic year 2025-2026 it has about 18,400 students and 2,950 employees (2,310 FTE).

## Mission and motto

The motto of the university is *Domi discimus, mundo serviamus* ("We learn at home, we serve the world"). Its strategy for 2025-2030, "Rooted and Open", has three pillars: small-scale, community-based education; open and reproducible science; and a sustainable campus that is climate neutral by 2035.

## Campuses

The university has two sites. The **Eemland Campus** (Science Park Eemland, 14 minutes by bicycle from Amersfoort Centraal) hosts FSE, FEB and FHLS, the University Library and most student housing. The **Stadspoort Site** in the historic city centre hosts FoL and FHSS, the Aula and the Executive Board offices. A free shuttle bus runs between the two sites every 15 minutes on weekdays from 07:30 to 19:00.
