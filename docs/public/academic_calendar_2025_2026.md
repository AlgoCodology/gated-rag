# Academic Calendar 2025-2026

The academic year at Home University runs from 1 September 2025 to 31 August 2026 and is divided into four blocks. Each block has eight weeks of teaching, followed by two weeks of examinations. Resits take place in a dedicated resit week at the end of the year, and for blocks 1 and 2 also in January.

## Key dates

| Block | Teaching period | Exam weeks | Results published by |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Block 1 | 1 September - 24 October 2025 | 27 October - 7 November 2025 | 28 November 2025 |
| Block 2 | 10 November 2025 - 16 January 2026 | 19 January - 30 January 2026 | 20 February 2026 |
| Block 3 | 2 February - 27 March 2026 | 30 March - 10 April 2026 | 8 May 2026 |
| Block 4 | 13 April - 5 June 2026 | 8 June - 19 June 2026 | 10 July 2026 |

The resit week for all blocks is **3 August - 7 August 2026**. Resit results are published by 28 August 2026.

## Official ceremonies

- **Opening of the Academic Year:** Monday 1 September 2025, 15:00, Aula (Stadspoort Site).
- **Dies Natalis (university anniversary):** Friday 10 October 2025. Lectures are cancelled from 13:00.
- **Graduation ceremonies:** 3 July 2026 (FSE, FEB, FHLS) and 10 July 2026 (FoL, FHSS).

## Enrolment and deadlines

- Enrolment for new bachelor's students closes on **1 May 2026** for the 2026-2027 academic year (1 April for programmes with a selection procedure).
- Students must register for each course through the portal at least **10 working days** before the start of the block, and for each exam at least **15 calendar days** before the exam date.
- Students who stop their studies must cancel their enrolment before the **1st of the month** to avoid paying tuition for the following month.
