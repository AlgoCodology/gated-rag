# Tuition Fees and Scholarships 2025-2026

## Tuition fees

Tuition at Home University depends on your nationality and on the programme level. All amounts are per academic year and in euros.

| Student category | Bachelor's | Master's |
|:---|---:|---:|
| EEA, Swiss and Surinamese students (statutory fee) | 2,601 | 2,601 |
| Non-EEA students, FSE and FHLS programmes | 15,800 | 18,900 |
| Non-EEA students, FEB programmes | 14,400 | 17,600 |
| Non-EEA students, FoL and FHSS programmes | 10,900 | 14,200 |

Students who are doing a second bachelor's or master's programme of the same level pay the institutional fee of 12,300 euros, even if they have EEA nationality, unless an exemption applies.

### Payment

Fees can be paid in a single instalment (due 1 September) or in ten monthly instalments from September to June. There is an administration fee of 25 euros for paying in instalments. Non-EEA students must pay the first instalment and a deposit of 3,000 euros before the visa application can start. The deposit is deducted from the final instalment.

### Other costs

Course books and materials cost on average 450 euros per year. Student housing on the Eemland Campus costs between 520 and 780 euros per month. The mandatory Dutch health insurance for international students costs about 135 euros per month.

## Scholarships

### Home Excellence Scholarship

The Home Excellence Scholarship is meant for outstanding non-EEA students starting a master's programme. Fifteen scholarships are awarded each year. The value is 10,000 euros, deducted from the tuition fee in the first year. Applications must be submitted by **1 February 2026** for programmes starting in September 2026. To apply you need a motivation letter, two references and a transcript with a GPA in the top 10% of your cohort.

### Rooted Fund for Dutch students

Dutch students who experience financial hardship (for example due to illness or caring responsibilities) can apply to the Rooted Fund for a grant of up to 1,500 euros. Applications are assessed by the student counsellors within 15 working days.

### Board scholarship for top athletes and artists

Students who compete at national level in sport or perform at a professional level in the arts can receive a study extension of up to one year and a financial contribution of 2,000 euros per year.

## Refunds

If a student cancels enrolment before 1 October, the tuition fee is refunded pro rata. After 1 February no refunds are given, except in case of serious illness proven by a doctor's statement.
