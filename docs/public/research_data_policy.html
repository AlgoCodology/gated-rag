Research Data Management Policy (2025)

This policy sets out how researchers at Home University handle, store and share research data, in line with the Netherlands Code of Conduct for Research Integrity and the FAIR principles.

1. Scope

The policy applies to all researchers, PhD candidates and research students working under the responsibility of Home University, irrespective of funding source.

2. Data management plan

Every research project must have a Data Management Plan (DMP). The DMP must be submitted to the faculty data steward within three months of the project start date and must be updated at least once a year. PhD candidates must hand in their DMP before the end of their first year.

3. Storage and retention

3.1 Storage

  • Active data must be stored on the university research drive (HomeStore), which is backed up daily.
  • Personal data must never be stored on private cloud services or unencrypted USB sticks.
  • Special categories of personal data (health, ethnicity, religion) require encryption at rest and approval from the faculty ethics committee.

3.2 Retention

Research data and the documentation needed to reproduce the results must be kept for a minimum of ten years after the last publication based on the data.

4. Open access and sharing

Data underlying a publication should be made available in a trusted repository (such as DANS or 4TU.ResearchData) under a licence that is as open as possible and as closed as necessary. Datasets receive a persistent identifier (DOI).

5. Roles

Principal investigators are responsible for the correct management of data in their projects. The faculty data stewards advise on DMPs and repositories. Questions can be sent to datasteward@homeuniversity.example.

6. Data breaches

Suspected loss of personal research data must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours.
